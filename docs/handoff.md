## Open items L2 must handle (from L1)

- **Plugin names.** `marv_gz_lockstep` / `marv::gz::Lockstep` in the generated SDF are provisional names.
- **Gazebo version.** Pin gz-sim 8 (Harmonic): gz-sim 8, 10 and others are installed, and `gz sdf` here is sdformat16.
  The CI image has no gz.
- **Step sizes.** The motor sub-step h, Gazebo's step and ticks per host step are chosen by §7.5 (SIM-7). `marv_plant`
  holds each command over one step call (zero-order hold), and nothing bounds dt/h yet.
- **Truth dispersal.** Truth is not dispersed (core §6), so every closed-loop result is perfect-model.
- **`thrust_max`.** blackbird.yaml gives 8.5 N per motor, but k·2800² = 12.25 N. The card uses only the speed range;
  B1 should look at this.
