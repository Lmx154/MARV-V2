A harness fix (owner decision 24's preference: fix the harness rather than add tolerance). Before it, the SIL saw
ω = 0 at execution 0 of a scenario that starts rotating, and then a step to ω0 at execution 1. Real hardware never
reads 0 rad/s from a tumbling body. Approved by Luis on 2026-10-03 (0014, fourth round, item 2).

## What changed

**The fix** (`sim/gz/plugin/src/lockstep.cpp`, PreUpdate).
- The cause (0003 item 11): PreUpdate reads before gz's physics step, and gz-sim 8 has no initial velocity, so the
  plugin applies the scenario's rates (`Link::SetAngularVelocity`) at the end of the step-0 PreUpdate. gz's step-0 read
  is therefore exactly 0.
- At host step 0 only, when the scenario gives initial body rates, the body passed to the command source and the adapter
  carries the scenario's FRD rates, copied bit for bit. Velocity, the other fields and every later step are unchanged.
- The log keeps gz's raw read in the gz fields; the plant-body fields are the body actually fed.
- Test-only negative control: `MARV_GZ_TEST_ZERO_FIRST_READ` set restores the old read (the `MARV_GZ_TEST_KEEP_VEL_CMD`
  pattern). The W4 evidence regeneration (below) reproduces the four pre-0016 gz logs byte for byte under it, so the
  switch is the old plugin exactly.

**New tests.**
- `tests/regression/quad/L02/gz/test_first_read.py` (T1; `ci/run_ci_gz.sh`, L2 group, step `gz_first_read`): on the

- `tests/regression/quad/L06/tools/test_rate_lead_hover_noise.py` (per push, 0.5 s): at hover the combined D + FF noise

## Why

The plant starts from (q0, ω0) at t = 0: gz's step-1 read is within 1.6e-5 rad/s of a torque-free body started there
(`L05/results/recovery_cause/README.md`, finding 1). The zero read was an artefact of when gz applies a velocity, not
the body's state. With the D term and the lag-compensated FF, the 0 → ω0 step became a large kick (0014 W4: the D
impulse about −0.58·Jω0 per axis). The PI loop was immune.
